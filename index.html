About

I design efficient AI systems that are easy to train and deploy, and secure by design. My work spans efficient training and inference, efficient and reliable AI agents, and privacy preserving AI. I am a Senior Research Engineer at Proofpoint and hold a PhD in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence from University College Cork.
